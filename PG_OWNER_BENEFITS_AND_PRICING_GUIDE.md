## 🌟 PG Owner Ko Kya Kya Fayda Hoga? (Key Benefits)

Jab aap PG owner se baat karein, toh unhe yeh 7 sabse bade fayde samjhayein:

### 1. Zero Brokerage & Direct Leads (100% Bachat)
- **Problem**: Local brokers har tenant ka 15 din se 1 mahine ka rent commission ke roop mein kaat lete hain.
- **PGSathi Fayda**: PGSathi par tenant seedha owner ko call ya WhatsApp karta hai. Ek rupaye ka bhi brokerage ya commission nahi dena padta.

### 2. Diary / Khata-Register Se Azaadi (Digital PG Manager)
- **Problem**: Diary mein rent likhna, bills kho jaana, kisne rent diya aur kisne nahi diya yaad na rehna.
- **PGSathi Fayda**: Mobile ya laptop se 1-click mein pata chal jata hai ki is mahine kitna rent collect hua, kiska rent baaki hai aur total kitne paise aane hain.

### 3. 1-Click WhatsApp Rent Reminder with UPI
- **Problem**: Har mahine tenants ke paas jaakar baar-baar rent maangna awkward aur mushkil hota hai.
- **PGSathi Fayda**: Dashboard mein ek click karte hi tenant ke WhatsApp par auto-filled message chala jaata hai jisme month, due amount aur owner ka UPI ID likha hota hai. Tenant turant payment kar deta hai.

### 4. Real-Time Bed & Room Inventory (Khali Beds Ki Jankari)
- Single, Double, Triple sharing rooms ka poora hisab.
- Kaun sa bed occupied hai aur kaun sa khali (vacant) hai, screen par saaf dikhta hai taaki naye tenant aate hi unhe turant room assign kiya ja sake.

### 5. Paperless Digital KYC & Tenant Onboarding
- Tenant ka Aadhaar Card, Student/Job ID, photo aur contact details system mein digitally aur safely save hoti hain.
- Na police verification mein file dhundhne ka jhanjhat, na papers kho jaane ka dar.

### 6. Complaint Management (Dispute-Free Operation)
- Agar PG mein geyser, AC, Wi-Fi ya plumbing kharab hai, toh tenant digitally complaint raise kar sakta hai.
- Owner ya warden status update kar sakte hain ("In Progress" ya "Resolved"), jisse tenants ke sath vivad (dispute) nahi hota.

### 7. Expense, Food/Mess & Profit/Loss Tracking
- Bijli ka bill, ration/grocery, staff salary, maintenance ka kharcha app mein note karein.
- Monthly profit & loss report milti hai taaki pata chale PG se actual kitni kamai ho rahi hai.
- Roz ka Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner menu bhi manage kar sakte hain.

## 📞 Telecalling Pitch & Calling Script (Support Executive Ke Liye)

Customer support executive is script ko follow karke PG owner se baat karein:

### Step 1: Opening (Namaskar & Connect)
> *"Namaskar sir/ma'am! Main **[Aapka Naam]** baat kar raha/rahi hu **PGSathi.in** se.*  
> *Sir, kya aapke paas 2 minute ka samay hai? Yeh call aapke PG business ko digitally manage karne aur bina kisi brokerage ke naye tenants paane ke baare mein hai."*

---

### Step 2: Pain Point Identify Karein
> *"Sir, abhi aap apne PG ka rent hisab, khali beds aur tenants ka record register/diary mein maintain karte hain ya kisi software mein?"*  
> *(Owner ka sunne ke baad)*  
> *"Sir, aksar PG owners ko 2 badi pareshaniyan aati hain:*  
> *1. Local brokers naye tenant lane par 15 din se 1 mahine ka rent brokerage ke naam par kaat lete hain.*  
> *2. Har mahine tenants se rent maangna, hisab yaad rakhna aur khali beds ko jaldi bharna mushkil ho jata hai."*

---

### Step 3: PGSathi Ka Solution & Free Trial Pitch Karein
> *"Sir, isi pareshani ko door karne ke liye humne banaya hai **PGSathi**!*  
> *Iske do sabse bade fayde hain:*  
> *1. **Zero Brokerage**: PGSathi par aapka PG list hoga aur verified tenants seedhe aapko call ya WhatsApp karenge bina kisi commission ke.*  
> *2. **Smart PG Manager**: Aapke mobile se hi ek click par pata chal jayega kiska rent pending hai, aur wahi se aap 1-click WhatsApp reminder bhej sakte hain apne UPI QR ke sath.*  
>  
> *Aur sabse achhi baat sir, abhi hum sabhi PG owners ko **15 Din Ka Bilkul Free Trial** de rahe hain. Isme koi payment nahi lagti, na koi credit card chahiye. Aap pehle 15 din chala kar dekhein!"*

---

### Step 4: Objection Handling (Agar Owner Sawal Kare)

#### Q1: "Mere paas toh pehle se diary hai, mujhe software ki kya zaroorat?"
> **Jawab**: *"Sir, diary mein kabhi hisab miss ho jata hai ya receipts kho jati hain. PGSathi mein aapke phone par ek jagah pata rehta hai ki is mahine kitna rent collect hua aur kitna baaki hai. Plus WhatsApp reminder feature se tenant turant payment kar dete hain aur aapko baar-baar maangna nahi padta."*

#### Q2: "Trial ke baad zabardasti paise katenge kya?"
> **Jawab**: *"Bilkul nahi sir! Hum sign up ke waqt koi card details nahi lete. 15 din poore hone ke baad aapki marzi hai. Agar aapko fayda lage toh aap plan le sakte hain, warna aapka free basic listing hamesha chalu rahega."*

#### Q3: "Pricing kitni hai agar main aage chalana chahu?"
> **Jawab**: *"Sir, single PG ke liye hamara Standard plan sirf **₹1,499 per month** ka hai jisme 50 tenants tak ka poora CRM, lead numbers aur reminders milte hain. Aur agar aap yearly lete hain toh sirf ₹14,990 padta hai jisme lagbhag 2 mahine bilkul free mil jate hain. All prices GST-included hain sir!"*

#### Q4: "Tenant ko koi app download karni padegi kya?"
> **Jawab**: *"Bilkul nahi sir! Tenant ke liye koi bhari app download karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Wo simple link se hi apna dues dekh sakte hain aur complaints raise kar sakte hain."*

---

### Step 5: Call Closing & Action (WhatsApp Link Bhejna)
> *"Sir, main abhi aapke WhatsApp par registration ka link bhej deta/deti hu. Aap bas 2 minute mein apna PG register kar lijiye. Humari team aapka verification complete karke 15 din ka free trial turant active kar degi.*  
> *Kya main is number par WhatsApp bhej du sir?"*  
> *(Owner: Haan bhej do)*  
> *"Thank you sir! PGSathi se judne ke liye aapka shukriya. Have a great day!"*
